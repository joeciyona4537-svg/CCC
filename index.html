Groundwater near the factory has NOT fallen.
Landesamt für Umwelt (LfU) Brandenburg · 52-well study (Sep 2020 – Aug 2026)

What the state measured: The State Environmental Agency tracked 52 monitoring wells over six years. The wells closest to the factory and Erkner showed a +8.5 cm rise in water levels.

What caused the regional dip? The 2021–24 drop (−32.5 cm) seen in news reports happened near the public water utility's own fields in Eggersdorf and Strausberg — over 15 km away, completely unrelated to Gigafactory operations.

Public Accountability Guarantee: All telemetry data is streamed directly to independent servers. An unedited summary is printed and delivered quarterly to all 9,200 households in Grünheide and Fangschleuse. Auditing agency chosen by Bürgerinitiative Grünheide.
